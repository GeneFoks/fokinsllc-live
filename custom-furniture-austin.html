Common Questions

Custom Furniture FAQ

Custom woodwork is priced by project — there's no standard rate because every piece is different. A wood slat accent wall typically runs $800–$2,500 depending on size and material. A live edge dining table can range from $1,200 to $4,000+. Gene gives you a written quote after your free consultation, with no obligation.
Timeline depends on complexity and Gene's current schedule — typically 1 to 4 weeks from deposit to delivery. Accent walls and shelving can often be done faster. Live edge tables and built-ins take longer. Gene gives you a realistic timeline upfront.
Gene works with cedar, walnut, pine, oak, live edge slabs, and more. For most projects he sources from local suppliers. If you have a specific wood in mind, just ask — he'll let you know if it works for your project and what it'll cost.
Absolutely. Send Gene a photo — Pinterest, Instagram, a website — and he'll tell you what it would take to build something similar for your space. Most designs can be adapted to your exact dimensions and wood preference.
Gene serves the entire greater Austin area — Cedar Park, Leander, Round Rock, Georgetown, Pflugerville, Austin, and surrounding areas. Custom woodwork orders are delivered and installed anywhere in the service area.
