Join the Fokins Crew

We're growing our team across Cedar Park, Leander, Round Rock, Georgetown & Austin. If you take pride in building things right, we want to hear from you.

Fokins Custom Woodwork is a family-run business known for quality craftsmanship — cedar fences, custom decks, fine woodwork, and dependable handyman work. As we take on more projects, we're looking for hardworking, reliable people to grow with us. Whether you're an experienced carpenter or just starting out and eager to learn the trade, there's a place on the crew.

Why work with Fokins

Steady local work

Consistent projects year-round, all within the Austin metro — no long commutes out of the area.

Learn real craftsmanship

Build fences, decks, and custom woodwork the right way, alongside Gene and an experienced crew.

Fair, on-time pay

Pay based on your experience and skills — and you get paid on time, every time.

Respected, family-run team

A small crew that treats each other and every customer with respect. Do good work, be valued.

Open positions

Roles across everything we build. Don't see an exact match? Apply anyway — we hire for attitude and work ethic.

Full-time / Seasonal

Fence & Gate Installer

Build cedar privacy fences, set posts in concrete, and hang gates that open smooth and last for years.

  • Fence & post experience a plus
  • Comfortable with post-hole & concrete work
  • Able to lift and work outdoors
Full-time / Seasonal

Deck & Carpentry Builder

Frame and build decks, porches, and pergolas — from footings to finished boards and railings.

  • Framing / carpentry experience preferred
  • Reads a tape and builds level & square
  • Owns basic hand & power tools (a plus)
Skilled

Custom Woodwork / Finish Carpenter

Craft built-ins, furniture, trim, and doors with clean, precise, finish-quality work.

  • Finish carpentry / cabinetry experience
  • Eye for detail and clean joints
  • Pride in a polished final product
Full-time

General Handyman

Handle doors, drywall, painting, TV mounts, small repairs, and installs across customer homes.

  • Broad handyman / repair skills
  • Good with customers and tidy on-site
  • Own transportation & basic tools
Entry level · No experience needed

Crew Helper / Laborer

Support the crew on every job and learn the trade hands-on. A great start if you're reliable and ready to work.

  • No experience required — we'll train
  • Strong work ethic and willing to learn
  • Show up on time, ready to go

What we look for

  • Reliability — you show up on time, every day
  • Pride in doing quality work
  • Own transportation to job sites
  • Respectful and professional with customers
  • Basic tools a plus (not required for helpers)
  • Legally authorized to work in the U.S.
  • English or Spanish — both welcome
  • Team player with a good attitude
