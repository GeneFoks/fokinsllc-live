Permits & Regulations
Per Cedar Park city code, fences up to 8 feet high do not require a building permit. Most residential privacy fences fall well under that limit. That said, your HOA may have its own rules around fence height, style, or materials — it's worth a quick check before we start. Gene will flag anything unusual during the estimate visit.
